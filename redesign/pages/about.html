About SNS Furniture

SNS Furniture is a fictitious business name operated by Sash & Shade.

SNS Furniture is a California custom furniture and interior design brand. We design furniture around your space and the way you live, offer a complimentary in-home design service, and curate stock furniture for living rooms, dining rooms, bedrooms, mattresses and accent spaces.

Furniture is delivered throughout California, and online checkout is available for California delivery addresses. Final specifications and pricing for custom furniture are confirmed during a design consultation.
