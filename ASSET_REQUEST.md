# SNS Furniture: asset request

The redesign is complete and ships with dignified fallbacks everywhere an asset is missing. The items below would **materially** improve it. Nothing here blocks review, and nothing is on the site as a placeholder: every slot already shows finished content that these assets would upgrade.

**Rights, for every item:** the image or video must be owned by SNS Furniture / Sash & Shade, or licensed for commercial web use, with model and property releases where people or private interiors appear. Please do not send imagery from other furniture brands, retailers or reference websites.

**Formats:** JPG or WebP for photography (we generate AVIF/WebP derivatives), MP4 (H.264) plus WebM for video, SVG for the logo. No transparency is needed unless stated.
