Privacy Policy

Effective: September 8, 2026

Sash & Shade respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit SNSFurniture.com, contact us, request a quote, place an order, make a payment, or otherwise interact with us.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including your name, billing and delivery addresses, email address, telephone number, product interests, order information, communications, and other information you choose to provide. We may also collect technical information such as IP address, browser or device information, pages viewed, referral information, cookies, and similar usage data.

Payment Information

Payment-card transactions may be processed by third-party payment processors and merchant-service providers, including Authorize.net or another processor selected by Sash & Shade. Sash & Shade does not need to store the full card number when payment information is transmitted directly to a payment processor. Payment processors handle payment information under their own security and privacy practices.

How We Use Information

We may use information to process orders and payments, arrange delivery, respond to inquiries, provide customer service, prevent fraud, maintain and improve our website, comply with legal obligations, send transactional communications, and—where permitted—send marketing communications. You may opt out of non-transactional marketing emails using the unsubscribe method provided in the message or by contacting us.

How We Share Information

We may share information with manufacturers, distributors, delivery providers, payment processors, website and technology providers, professional advisers, and other service providers when reasonably necessary to operate our business or fulfill your order. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a business transaction. We do not sell payment-card information.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies for core site operation, preferences, analytics, security, and marketing. Browser settings may allow you to limit cookies, although doing so can affect site functionality.

Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including order, tax, accounting, warranty, fraud-prevention, and legal requirements.

California Privacy Rights

California residents may have rights regarding certain personal information under applicable California law. Depending on the law and circumstances, these can include rights to know, access, correct, or delete certain information and to receive information about qualifying disclosures. Requests can be submitted to info@snsfurniture.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Children

SNSFurniture.com is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above indicates when this version became effective.
